Forever Gold: how the addon would look

Mockups in the game's Classic-style look, matched to your tooltips and frames. Hide, leather, cloth and enchanting prices are real (EU Alliance PvP, TSM scan 2026-11-12 12:25 UTC). The green item and its disenchant maths are an example. Nothing here posts, buys or sends on its own.

  • The addon reads and shows. It never posts, buys, cancels, sends mail or crafts. A click on a row runs one auction house search at most.
  • Prices and calls come from the website, through a small helper on your PC that copies a file into the addon folder. New advice shows after a /reload or the next login.
  • The addon also writes your bags, bank, mail, sales and recipes to its saved file at logout, so the website learns what you actually own and sold.
